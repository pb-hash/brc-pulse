MYBRC

Your balance, your mining and your payouts, worked out from the whole chain and kept up to date block by block. Paste a wallet address: 64 letters and numbers, shown in your BrowserCoin wallet. Never paste your private key. No BRC Works page asks for it, except BRC Vault, to back it up.

Your address never leaves your browser in full: the page downloads a small file of addresses that start with the same three characters and finds yours on your device. It's remembered on this device.

                  BRC Works
                  
      
                  Small, independent tools for BrowserCoin, safe by design. Each one is a single web page that does all its work inside your browser.
                  
    
                  
    
                    
      
                  • Your secrets never leave your device. Nothing you paste, type or open is uploaded. There's no account, no tracking, and no server that ever sees your keys, backups, files or letters.
                    • 
      
                  • Only BRC Vault ever asks for a key that holds coins, and only to lock it into a password-protected backup, right there on your device. If any other page, person or message asks for your key, it isn't us. Never paste it.
                    • 
      
                  • Everything else needs only public things: addresses, signed messages and receipts, which are safe to share. BRC Post uses its own mailbox key, which never holds coins.
                    • 
      
                  • You don't have to take our word for it. Every page is open source. BRC Vault is locked so it can't connect to anything at all; BRC Post can reach only the public drand time service. Both work with Wi‑Fi off.
