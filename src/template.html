MYBRCLook up your balance and mining

MYBRC

Your balance, your mining and your payouts, worked out from the whole chain and kept up to date block by block. Paste a wallet address: 64 letters and numbers, shown in your BrowserCoin wallet. Never paste your private key, here or anywhere.

Your address never leaves your browser in full: the page downloads a small file of addresses that start with the same three characters and finds yours on your device. It's remembered on this device.

Check a stamp.

A stamp proves a file existed by a certain date, using the BrowserCoin chain. Paste the stamp receipt made in BRC Seal. Add the file too, to check it is the same one. The file is read here and never uploaded. A message signed on the chain, or a mailbox link, is checked here too: paste the whole block.

              Straight from the network
              Your browser reads the public helper servers directly and checks for new blocks every 6 seconds. No middleman, no server of ours.
              
    
              No account, nothing tracked
              No sign-up, no cookies and no analytics. The page asks the network about blocks and nothing about you.
              
    
              One file
              The whole dashboard is a single page. Save it, host it anywhere, or open it from your desktop, and it still runs live.
